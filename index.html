Our Story

Redefining Reconditioned Luxury in Nepal

Founded in Tinkune, Kathmandu, RAAZ Auto was established to fill a crucial void in the Nepalese pre-owned automotive market: **trust and transparency**.

We specialize in high-end SUVs, premium pickups, and electric vehicles. By combining mechanical inspection workflows with customer care, we ensure that buying a reconditioned car feels exactly like buying brand-new.

Company Milestones

2021 — Operations Launched

RAAZ Auto opened its main showroom doors in Tinkune with a curation of 5 premium diesel SUVs.

2023 — Certified Workshop Hub

Built the Lalitpur service hub equipped with advanced diagnostics scanners, introducing the 150-Point Certified Audit.

2025 — EV Dealership Pioneer

Imported and certified the first batch of pre-owned electric SUVs, helping Nepali drivers adopt green vehicle models.
